# CA Inter Pro - ICAI Master Exam Suite & AI Evaluator

A comprehensive preparation suite designed specifically for **ICAI CA Intermediate** (New Scheme) students across all 6 papers. Built with React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Vite, and an intelligent ICAI Descriptive Answer Evaluator.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. All 6 ICAI Intermediate Papers (New Scheme)
- **Group 1**:
  - **Paper 1**: Advanced Accounting (AS 1 to 29, Buyback, Reconstruction, Amalgamation, CFS)
  - **Paper 2**: Corporate and Other Laws (Companies Act Sec 1-148, LLP Act, General Clauses, Interpretation, FEMA)
  - **Paper 3**: Taxation (Section A: Income Tax Law - Sec 115BAC, Heads of Income; Section B: GST - Supply, RCM, ITC Sec 16/17(5))
- **Group 2**:
  - **Paper 4**: Cost and Management Accounting (Standard Costing Variances, Marginal, Process, Overheads, ABC)
  - **Paper 5**: Auditing and Ethics (SAs, SQC 1, Risk Assessment, Evidence, Company Audit, Audit Report SA 700/705)
  - **Paper 6**: Financial Management and Strategic Management (FM: Capital Budgeting, WACC, Leverages; SM: Ansoff, BCG, Porter)

---

### 2. Intelligent Descriptive Answer Evaluator
- Write complete answers directly in an exam-grade typing workspace with quick heading inserts (`Statutory Provisions`, `Working Notes`, `Conclusion`).
- Instant automated evaluation based on **ICAI Step Marking Scheme**:
  - **Statutory Citations Detection**: Verifies exact Sections (e.g. Sec 135, Sec 16(2)) and Standards (e.g. AS 16, SA 705).
  - **Technical Keyword Matching**: Assesses mandatory legal, accounting, and taxation phrases.
  - **Working Notes Assessment**: Rewards intermediate computation steps.
  - **ICAI Examiner Comments & Tips**: Highlights why marks were deducted and compares against the official ICAI Model Answer side-by-side.

---

### 3. Strict Smart Repetition Engine (User Rule Enforced)
- **High-Importance / Examiner Favorite Questions**:
  - Tagged `importance: 'HIGH'` (e.g., Sec 135 CSR, AS 16 Borrowing Costs, Standard Costing Variances, SA 705 Opinions, ITC Sec 16(2)).
  - Kept in a continuous spaced repetition review loop (re-tested if answered incorrectly or after spaced review intervals).
- **Low-Importance / One-Off Questions**:
  - Tagged `importance: 'LOW'`.
  - **Permanently retired** once answered! Never repeated in future practice sessions.

---

### 4. Unlimited Dynamic Question Generator
- Algorithmic engine synthesizing infinite ICAI exam question variants with randomized values, complete step-marking rubrics, and model solutions:
  - Standard Costing 5-way Material Variances
  - Section 115BAC Default Tax Regime Computation
  - CSR Section 135 & Ongoing Projects
  - SA 705 Audit Materiality & Opinion Formulation
  - Capital Budgeting CFAT, Salvage & NPV
  - AS 16 Borrowing Cost Capitalization

- ICAI standard Question Palette with Answered (Green), Not Answered (Red), Review (Purple), and Not Visited (Gray) indicators.
