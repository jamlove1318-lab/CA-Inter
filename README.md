## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. All 6 ICAI Intermediate Papers (New Scheme)
* **Group 1**:
  * **Paper 1: Advanced Accounting** (AS 1 to 29, Buyback u/s 68, Internal Reconstruction, Amalgamation AS 14, CFS)
  * **Paper 2: Corporate and Other Laws** (Companies Act 2013 Sec 1–148, LLP Act 2008, General Clauses Act, Interpretation of Statutes, FEMA 1999)
  * **Paper 3: Taxation** (Income Tax Sec 115BAC Default Regime, Heads of Income; GST Supply, RCM, ITC Sec 16 & 17(5))
* **Group 2**:
  * **Paper 4: Cost & Management Accounting** (Standard Costing Variances, Marginal Costing, Process, ABC, Overheads)
  * **Paper 5: Auditing & Ethics** (Standards on Auditing, SQC 1, Risk Assessment, Evidence SA 500, SA 705 Opinions, CARO 2020)
  * **Paper 6: Financial Management & Strategic Management** (FM: Capital Budgeting, WACC, Leverages; SM: Ansoff Matrix, BCG, Porter)

---

### 2. Intelligent Descriptive Answer Evaluator
* Type descriptive answers directly with step-by-step headings (`Statutory Provisions`, `Case Application`, `Working Notes`, `Conclusion`).
* Instant automated grading based on **ICAI Step Marking Scheme**:
  * **Statutory Citations Detection**: Verifies exact Sections (e.g. Sec 135, Sec 16(2)) and Standards (e.g. AS 16, SA 705).
  * **Technical Keywords Check**: Green for matched keywords, red for missed keywords.
  * **Working Notes Assessment**: Awards step marks for intermediate calculations.
  * **ICAI Examiner Feedback**: Compares side-by-side with official ICAI Suggested Answers.

---

### 3. Smart Repetition Engine (Strict Rules Enforced)
* **High-Importance Questions**: Repeat based on spaced review intervals or when answered incorrectly.
* **Low-Importance Questions**: **Permanently retired** once attempted. Never repeat in future sessions.

---

### 4. Unlimited Dynamic Question Generator
* Algorithmic question variation engine synthesizing infinite new ICAI problems with randomized numbers, model solutions, and step rubrics:
  * Standard Costing 5-Way Variances
  * Section 115BAC Default Tax Regime Computation
  * CSR Section 135 & Ongoing Projects
  * SA 705 Materiality & Modification of Opinion
  * Capital Budgeting CFAT & Salvage
  * AS 16 Borrowing Cost Capitalization

---

### 5. 100-Mark ICAI Exam Simulator
* 3 Hours + **15-Minute ICAI Reading Time Mode** (paper viewable with locked inputs, simulating 1:45 PM – 2:00 PM examination).
* Standard ICAI color-coded Question Palette: Green (Answered), Red (Not Answered), Purple (Review), Gray (Not Visited).

---

### 6. Case Study Lab
* Integrated 15–30 mark multi-part business cases (e.g. *Apex Agrochem Ltd*, *Starlight Electronics Ltd*) with linked MCQs and descriptive questions.

---

### 7. Past Papers & RTP Vault
* Real exam papers (May 2024, Nov 2023, May 2023, Nov 2022), RTPs (Nov 2024, May 2024), and MTPs with examiner observations.

---

### 8. 🎮 CA Game Zone (Play & Learn)
* **Section Blitz**: 60-second rapid-fire card matching with streak multipliers.
* **ICAI Examiner Boss Battle**: Defeat the Chief Examiner by answering tricky questions; use 50:50 lifelines to protect your 3 hearts.
* **Adjustment Detective**: Rapid classification of PGBP, Cash Flow, and Cost Sheet adjustments into *Add*, *Deduct*, or *Ignore*.
* Pure Web Audio API synthesized arcade sounds (zero external audio files needed).
